webkick – top-down football in your browser

A fast, skill-based football game. The ball runs free in front of your player: run into it to dribble, press fire just after a touch to shoot, hold fire to trap and pass, pull back to lob, and bend shots with aftertouch. Play single matches against the computer on three skill levels, or practise shooting and penalties.
